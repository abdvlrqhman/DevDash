Square Kufic draws Arabic letters on a grid of squares. A terminal draws text on a grid of cells. The mark is the letter د (dal) built from grid squares, with a brass cursor block inside it: it reads as د and as a prompt. Verdigris and brass come from copper lamps that have aged; they sit on a green-black ink instead of plain black. Readex Pro covers Latin and Arabic in one family, so Arabic names and notes look native. Monospace appears only where there is code.
